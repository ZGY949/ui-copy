# Source notes

This skill independently organizes decisions and examples for interface content, informed by the official design guides below. It does not reproduce their prose, screenshots, or visual assets, and does not claim endorsement. The linked guides and associated trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

These references support content decisions; they do not establish a fixed priority across platforms. Follow the product's own platform requirements, facts, and voice. Consult the original source when platform details or changed guidance matter.

| Official source | Relevant ideas | Do not transfer mechanically |
|---|---|---|
| Apple [Writing](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/writing) | Screen tasks, information order, action-oriented language, patterns, errors, and tone | English capitalization, sentence structures, or specific Apple platform conventions |
| Apple [Alerts](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/alerts) | Objects, consequences, and actions in confirmations and alerts | Control counts, appearance, or mandatory behavior on another platform |
| IBM Carbon [Content](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/content) | Consistent terms, understandable and actionable text, accessible content | IBM-specific terminology, English grammar, or brand rules |
| IBM Carbon [Button guidelines](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/core/components/button/guidelines) | Predictable actions, action and object, contextual exceptions | A fixed phrase structure, alignment, or capitalization for every button |
| IBM Carbon [Empty states](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/core/patterns/empty-states) | Distinguish no data, no results, errors, and quiet states requiring no action | Desktop layouts or an action in every empty state |
| Ant Design [Copywriting (Chinese)](https://ant.design/docs/spec/copywriting-cn/) | User perspective, stable terminology, important information first, and Chinese wording | Treating a component's default wording as universally best |

References assembled on October 7, 2026. Links and platform details can change. For a specific platform requirement, use the current official source. Linked third-party material may be available in its original language.
